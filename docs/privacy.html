tiptv
Privacy Policy
Last Updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Privacy-First Design: tiptv does not collect, monitor, track, store on remote servers, or sell any personal data, analytics, or user identifiers.

tiptv is a local IPTV media player application developed for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. The application functions exclusively as a player client for media URLs and playlists supplied directly by the user.

2. Information Collection and Storage

tiptv collects no personal data:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, sign in, or provide an email address to use tiptv.
  • Local Storage Only: All playlists, Xtream Codes credentials, channel favorites, watch history, hidden channel configurations, and playback settings are stored exclusively on your device.
  • No Remote Tracking: We do not operate user-tracking servers, database backends, or telemetry systems.

3. Network Connections & Streaming Content

tiptv is strictly a player software client:

  • tiptv does not host, provide, distribute, index, or bundle any stream content, playlist channels, or video files.
  • When playing media or fetching Electronic Program Guide (EPG) data, your device connects directly to the IPTV server or URL you provided. No video or audio traffic is routed through, intercepted by, or logged on our servers.
  • Users are solely responsible for obtaining and using only legally authorized content.

4. In-App Purchases and Payment Processing

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed directly through Apple's App Store using StoreKit. tiptv never collects, sees, or retains your credit card information, bank account details, or billing address.

5. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

tiptv contains no third-party advertising SDKs and no third-party behavioral analytics frameworks. We do not sell or share any information with data brokers or advertising networks.

6. App Store Privacy Nutrition Label

In compliance with Apple App Store requirements, tiptv declares Data Not Collected. The app does not track users across apps and websites owned by other companies.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via GitHub or through the app's support channel.
